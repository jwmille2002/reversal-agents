Antiplatelet reversal

Aspirin · Clopidogrel · Prasugrel · Ticagrelor
Reversal strategy Reversal is controversial. Consider platelet transfusion if any of: neurosurgery, ventriculostomy, large ICH, or acute neurologic decline with intracranial hemorrhage. For intracranial hemorrhage or surgical intervention (e.g. CABG), consider TXA/EACA or desmopressin.

Warfarin reversal

Vitamin K antagonist
Reversal strategy Preferred agent: 4F-PCC, fixed dose or INR/weight-based dose. Vitamin K 1–10 mg IV/PO for all patients. Administer vitamin K before administering PCC.

DOAC (Anti-Xa) reversal

Rivaroxaban · Apixaban · Edoxaban
Reversal strategy Preferred agent: andexanet alfa. Alternative agent: 4F-PCC (weight-based dose or fixed dose).

Direct thrombin inhibitors

Dabigatran · Argatroban · Bivalirudin · Fondaparinux
Reversal strategy Dabigatran — preferred agent: idarucizumab. Alternative agents: rFVIIa, FEIBA, or dialysis (4F-PCC also listed below). Argatroban/bivalirudin have a short half-life; a reversal agent is not recommended.

Heparin derivative reversal

Unfractionated heparin (UFH) · Low-molecular-weight heparin (LMWH)
